Mycorrhizal is the Android client for a self-hosted personal relationship
manager. You run the backend on your own server; the app talks only to it.
Nothing is sent to a third party, and there is no account to create with us.

Your contacts are kept in a neutral contact model, so the same data can be
imported from and exported to the formats you already use.

Features:

* Contacts with multiple names, phone numbers, email addresses, postal
  addresses, URLs, birthdays, and personal information, plus notes, photos,
  and tags.
* Relationships between people, and circles for the groups they belong to.
* A timeline of life events, activities, and reminders per contact.
* Reach-out cadence: pick how often you want to stay in touch, and the app
  reminds you when someone is due.
* Encryption and local app lock: optional biometric unlock on the device,
  with a single-root encrypted offline mirror so the app keeps working
  without a connection.
* CardDAV and CalDAV sync, so your phone's built-in contacts and calendar
  apps can use the same data.
* Optional, opt-in call and SMS activity logging (never the message body),
  processed on your own server.
* Two-factor authentication and per-device sessions, with recovery codes.

This build is the F-Droid variant: it contains no Firebase or Google Play
Services code. Reminder notifications are delivered by the app's own
on-device scheduler.

Mycorrhizal is free and open source software (MIT). A backend is required;
see the project's website for deployment instructions.
